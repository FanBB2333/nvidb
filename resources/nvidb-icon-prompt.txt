Use case: logo-brand.
Create one original finished macOS app icon for nvidb, a professional developer tool for GPU cluster monitoring and compute-job scheduling.
Presentation shared across this candidate series: one centered icon only on a square canvas. A dark charcoal-green rounded-square tile occupies 86% of the canvas, with truly transparent margins outside it. The central symbol occupies roughly 60% of the full canvas width. Large smooth corners, restrained very subtle tile illumination, almost flat graphic design, extremely crisp edges and high contrast. Green or mint symbol on the dark tile. Clear optical balance and generous padding, easy to recognize at notification-icon size.
No text, labels, wordmark, mockup, UI, watermark, tiny details, thin wires, busy circuit traces, exaggerated 3D rendering or glow. Do not resemble NVIDIA's eye or another existing tech brand. Output a single usable app icon, not a design sheet.

Candidate D — NV MONOGRAM.
Design an exceptionally refined angular NV monogram as one custom solid graphic. Two broad diagonal strokes and two short upright strokes interlock; a sharp V-shaped negative-space cut in the middle makes the N and V share one compact silhouette. Invent a coherent geometric mark rather than typesetting the two letters. Use bold clean strokes with slightly softened outer corners and precise acute interior angles. Flat warm-white for the principal mark with one small mint-green terminal segment integrated into it. No ribbon loops, no nodes, no chip. Premium minimalist software identity, memorable and highly legible.
